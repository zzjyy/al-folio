Founding Rector of ZZOK and head of the algorithmic logic group. Tomasz earned his PhD at the University of Warsaw (2001), defended his habilitation at the Polish Academy of Sciences (2011), and was awarded the title of full professor in 2019.

His research lies in proof complexity, descriptive complexity, and the algorithmics of constraint-satisfaction problems. He has held visiting appointments at the University of Cambridge (DAMTP, 2009–2010) and ETH Zürich (2017–2018), and currently leads the NCN OPUS-22 project *Proof complexity of CSPs over finite structures*.

Outside the office Tomasz is an avid sailor; the college's annual end-of-year regatta on the Dąbie lagoon was his idea.
