## Visit us

> **ZZOK &mdash; Kolegium Informatyki i Kognitywistyki**
> ul. Słoneczna 27
> 70-001 Szczecin
> Poland

The main building (A) sits a ten-minute walk from Szczecin Główny railway station, served by the 71 and 75 tram lines (stop: *Słoneczna*). Visitors should report to the porter's lodge for a day pass.

## Get in touch

For admissions, research collaboration, media enquiries, public-information requests, accessibility advice, or anything else, please write to **<code>office@zzok.edu.pl</code>** and the message will be routed to the right office. We answer within two working days during term.

## Opening hours

The Rector's office and Chancellor's office are open **Monday–Friday, 09:00–16:00 CET**, except on Polish public holidays and during the institutional break (24–31 December). The library is open Mon–Fri 08:00–20:00 and Saturdays 10:00–14:00 during term.

## Data protection

ZZOK is a controller of personal data within the meaning of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR). For data-protection enquiries please write to <code>office@zzok.edu.pl</code> with the subject line "IOD"; the message will be forwarded to the Data Protection Officer. Information on processing of applicant and student data is published in the [Public Information Bulletin (BIP)](/about/#bip).

## Whistleblowing

A confidential channel established under the Polish *Whistleblower Protection Act* of 14 June 2024 is available at <code>https://zzok.edu.pl/sygnalista</code>. Reports may be submitted in Polish or English, with or without identification.
