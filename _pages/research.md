ZZOK organises its research activity around **six clusters**. Each cluster is led by a senior academic, meets weekly during term, and is open to MSc and PhD students by invitation.

## Research clusters

### 1. Algorithmic Foundations and Theory
*Lead: Prof. dr hab. Tomasz Wieczorek.* Proof complexity, descriptive complexity, constraint satisfaction, parametrised algorithms. The cluster co-organises the Szczecin–Greifswald Logic Day (annual).

### 2. Distributed and Reliable Systems
*Lead: Dr hab. inż. Marek Janicki, prof. ZZOK.* Byzantine fault tolerance, deterministic replay, geo-replicated storage. Current industry collaborations with two clearing operators and one shipping-logistics platform headquartered in Szczecin's port district.

### 3. Human–Computer Interaction
*Lead: Mgr inż. Piotr Dąbrowski.* Accessible interfaces, mixed-reality interaction, public-sector service design. The HCI lab (room A-302) hosts user-study sessions every Thursday during term.

### 4. Computational Cognitive Science
*Lead: Dr hab. Joanna Krzemińska, prof. ZZOK.* Predictive coding, Bayesian models of perception, electrophysiological recording in primary sensory cortex. The group operates a small EEG facility (32-channel) shared with the Faculty of Cognitive Studies.

### 5. Applied Machine Learning
*Co-leads: Dr hab. inż. Marek Janicki and Dr hab. Joanna Krzemińska.* Sample-efficient learning, calibration and uncertainty, model auditing. Joint MSc track with the Faculty of Cognitive Studies.

### 6. Formal Methods and Cybersecurity
*Lead: Dr Halina Wójcik.* Separation logic, weak-memory verification, side-channel analysis. Membership of the CISPA &mdash; ZZOK joint colloquium since 2024.
