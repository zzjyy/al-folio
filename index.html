ZZOK is an independent, state-accredited college of higher education established in 2006 and located in the harbour city of Szczecin, on Poland’s western coast. Our two faculties — Computer Science and Cognitive Studies — offer accredited Bachelor’s, Master’s and doctoral programmes to roughly 1,400 students drawn from across Poland and the Baltic region.

The college is registered in the national register of non-public higher-education institutions kept by the Ministry of Science and Higher Education under entry 376, and our programmes are externally evaluated by the Polish Accreditation Committee (PKA). We are a signatory of the Erasmus Charter for Higher Education 2021–2027 and a member institution of the European University Association.

Research at ZZOK is concentrated in six clusters: algorithmic foundations, distributed systems, human–computer interaction, computational cognitive science, applied machine learning, and formal methods for cybersecurity. Our staff publish in venues such as POPL, NeurIPS, Cognition and ACM Transactions on Computer Systems, and the college hosts the annual Baltic Workshop on Computation and Mind, now in its eleventh edition.

Prospective students can browse our programmes of study, meet our faculty, explore research at ZZOK, or read the admissions guide. The next intake opens 1 June 2026.

selected publications

  1. LICS
    On the proof complexity of constraint satisfaction over finite structures
    Tomasz Wieczorek and Halina Wójcik
    In Proceedings of the 41st Annual ACM/IEEE Symposium on Logic in Computer Science (LICS), 2026
    Abs Bib

    We give the first matched upper and lower bounds for resolution refutations of CSPs over arbitrary finite relational structures, settling an open question of Atserias and Bulatov. The proof relies on a novel pebble-game argument over the universal-algebraic invariants of the template.

  2. Cognition
    Predictive coding in primary auditory cortex: a hierarchical Bayesian account
    Joanna Krzemińska and Aleksandra Nowak
    Cognition, 2026
    Abs Bib

    We present electrophysiological evidence from 38 adult listeners supporting a hierarchical Bayesian account of predictive coding in primary auditory cortex. The model accounts for both phase-locked responses and adaptation effects within a single generative framework.

  3. ACM TOCS
    A deterministic-replay key-value store for high-throughput clearing systems
    Marek Janicki and Piotr Dąbrowski
    In ACM Transactions on Computer Systems, 2026
    Abs Bib

    We describe a geo-replicated key-value store deployed in production by a European financial-clearing operator. The system provides deterministic replay across replicas at sub-millisecond tail latency and tolerates simultaneous failures of f out of 3f+1 replicas.
