Entry requirements

Bachelor’s (BSc) — Computer Science / Cognitive Science

  • Polish świadectwo dojrzałości (matura) or a recognised equivalent (IB, European Baccalaureate, A-levels, GCE, etc.).
  • For Computer Science: matura mathematics at the extended (rozszerzona) level recommended; minimum 50% on the basic level.
  • Proof of English at B2 (e.g. IELTS 6.0, TOEFL iBT 80, Cambridge FCE) for applicants whose matura does not include English.

Master’s (MSc)

  • A Bachelor’s degree (Licencjat, Inżynier, or equivalent) in a related discipline.
  • Transcripts; one academic reference; a 500-word statement of motivation.
  • Proof of English at B2+ / C1.

Doctoral (PhD) — through the Szkoła Doktorska ZZOK

  • A Master’s degree (or equivalent) in a related discipline; transcripts and two academic references.
  • A research proposal (max. 8 pages) and a prospective supervisor agreement.
  • Entrance interview with the doctoral school committee.

Scholarships

ZZOK administers four scholarship schemes:

  1. Rector’s merit scholarship — top 10% of each cohort, 4 000 PLN per semester.
  2. Need-based grant — administered jointly with the Ministry, on application.
  3. Voivodeship Scholarship for International Students — funded by the West Pomeranian Voivodeship; 12 places per year.
  4. NAWA STER — applicable to PhD candidates from outside the EU.

A separate fund is available for students with disabilities; please contact the Dean of Students at dean-of-students@zzok.edu.pl for confidential advice.

How to apply

Applications are submitted through the IRK ZZOK portal at https://irk.zzok.edu.pl. The portal accepts both Polish ePUAP / mObywatel authentication and ordinary e-mail registration. Required documents (in PDF) are uploaded directly; originals are presented at enrolment.

For questions please write to admissions@zzok.edu.pl or call the admissions office on +48 91 555 0148 (Mon–Fri, 09:00–15:00 CET).
