faculty

Senior academic staff across the Faculties of Computer Science and Cognitive Studies.

The list below covers our tenured and habilitated staff. The full directory of lecturers, postdoctoral researchers and visiting fellows is maintained on the staff intranet and is available on request to office@zzok.edu.pl.

Faculty of Computer Science

Prof. dr hab. Tomasz Wieczorek — Rector; Algorithmic Logic

Tomasz joined ZZOK as a founding member in 2006. He holds a habilitation from the Polish Academy of Sciences (2011) and was awarded the title of full professor in 2019. His research concerns proof complexity, descriptive set theory, and the foundations of constraint satisfaction. He has held visiting positions at the University of Cambridge and ETH Zürich. Recent funding: NCN OPUS-22 (PI, 2023–2026).

  • Office: B-114 · Hours: Tuesdays 14:00–15:30 · Email: wieczorek@zzok.edu.pl

Dr hab. inż. Marek Janicki, prof. ZZOK — Dean; Distributed Systems

Marek leads the Distributed and Reliable Systems group. His work on Byzantine-fault-tolerant consensus has been deployed in production by two European financial-clearing operators. He chairs the curriculum committee for the BSc in Computer Science.

  • Office: B-208 · Hours: Mondays 10:00–11:30 · Email: janicki@zzok.edu.pl

Dr Halina Wójcik — Senior Lecturer; Formal Methods & Cybersecurity

Halina convenes the Formal Methods Reading Group and supervises the joint MSc track with the Łukasiewicz Research Network. Her dissertation (Warsaw, 2018) established new completeness results for separation logic over weak-memory models.

  • Office: B-117 · Hours: Thursdays 11:00–12:30 · Email: wojcik@zzok.edu.pl

Mgr inż. Piotr Dąbrowski — Senior Lecturer; Human–Computer Interaction

Piotr is doctoral candidate (defence scheduled spring 2026) and runs the undergraduate HCI lab. Before joining ZZOK he spent six years at a Tri-City product studio working on accessible interfaces for public-sector clients.

  • Office: A-302 · Hours: by appointment · Email: dabrowski@zzok.edu.pl

Faculty of Cognitive Studies

Dr hab. Joanna Krzemińska, prof. ZZOK — Vice-Rector for Research; Computational Neuroscience

Joanna’s lab combines electrophysiological recording with Bayesian modelling of perception. In 2026 she was elected to the Committee on Neurobiology of the Polish Academy of Sciences.

  • Office: C-205 · Hours: Wednesdays 13:00–14:30 · Email: krzeminska@zzok.edu.pl

Dr hab. Aleksandra Nowak, prof. ZZOK — Dean; Psycholinguistics

Aleksandra works on the cognitive representation of grammatical aspect, with field studies on Polish, Russian and Lithuanian. Her group runs the longitudinal Baltic Child Language Project (2019–).

  • Office: C-118 · Hours: Fridays 09:00–10:30 · Email: nowak@zzok.edu.pl
