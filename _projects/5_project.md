A two-year Master's bridging cognitive science and engineering practice. Graduates design and evaluate systems whose users are human — assistive technology, accessible interfaces, decision-support tools, and human-in-the-loop ML applications.

## Curriculum
- Experimental design and statistics for HCI
- Mixed-reality and embodied interaction
- Computational models of attention and decision-making
- Accessibility engineering and inclusive design
- Year-long supervised thesis project (30 ECTS)

## Joint supervision

Most theses are co-supervised by one member of the Faculty of Cognitive Studies and one member of the Faculty of Computer Science. The arrangement is institutional policy and has been in place since the programme's launch in 2017.
