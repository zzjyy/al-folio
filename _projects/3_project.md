The seven-semester engineering programme (210 ECTS) qualifies graduates for the protected Polish professional title *inżynier*. Compared with the three-year BSc, students take an additional engineering-project semester and an extended industrial-placement module.

## Industrial placement

A 12-week supervised placement at one of ZZOK's 28 partner companies is a mandatory part of the curriculum. Placements are coordinated by the Faculty Industry Officer (industry@zzok.edu.pl) and are normally undertaken in the summer between the third and fourth years.

## Specialisations
- Distributed systems and reliability
- Cybersecurity
- Data-intensive systems

The programme is accredited by the Polish Accreditation Committee (PKA) and recognised across the European Higher Education Area under the Bologna framework.
