The PhD in Computer Science is delivered through the **Szkoła Doktorska ZZOK** (the institutional doctoral school established in 2019 under the Polish Higher Education Act of 2018). Successful applicants receive a four-year stipend at the rate set by the Ministry of Science and Higher Education.

## Admission
- Applications open 1 April; deadline 15 May.
- A prospective supervisor agreement is required at application.
- Shortlisted applicants are interviewed by the doctoral-school committee in early June.

## Programme structure

The first year is dominated by coursework (24 ECTS of advanced electives, including the compulsory module *Foundations of research practice*). Years two to four are predominantly thesis research. Doctoral candidates are required to publish at least two papers in venues ranked B or higher on the Polish Ministry's list before submission of the dissertation.

## Available supervisors (2026/27)

Wieczorek (algorithmic logic) · Janicki (distributed systems) · Wójcik (formal methods). Co-supervision with members of the Faculty of Cognitive Studies is encouraged for interdisciplinary topics.
