A research-oriented MSc designed for graduates intending to pursue doctoral study or technical leadership positions in industry. Compared with the MSc in Applied AI, the curriculum places greater emphasis on theoretical computer science and systems research.

## Specialisations
- Algorithms and theory
- Distributed and reliable systems
- Formal methods and security

A Polish-language track exists for Polish citizens and EU/EEA students who hold a *świadectwo dojrzałości* with Polish; the English-language and Polish-language cohorts share the algorithms-and-theory specialisation but have separate cohorts for the other two specialisations.

The MSc thesis (30 ECTS) must be defended before a three-member committee, of which at least one member is external to the Faculty.
