The interdisciplinary BSc in Cognitive Science draws on philosophy, psychology, linguistics, neuroscience and computer science. Students complete a core curriculum in their first two years and choose a *specialisation track* in their third year: **cognitive neuroscience**, **psycholinguistics**, or **human-centred computing**.

## Programme structure

| Year | Core modules | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Introduction to cognitive science; Foundations of psychology; Logic and argumentation; Introduction to programming; Anatomy & physiology of the nervous system | Joint cohort with BSc CS for the programming module |
| 2 | Experimental methods; Statistical inference; Philosophy of mind; Linguistic structure; Computational models of cognition | Each student joins a research group for one semester |
| 3 | Specialisation track; Final-year thesis | 20-ECTS thesis required |

Graduates are eligible to apply for our MSc in Cognitive Systems Engineering or for MSc-level cognitive science programmes across the European Higher Education Area.
