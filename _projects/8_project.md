The PhD in Cognitive Sciences is offered jointly by the Faculty of Cognitive Studies and (for computationally oriented topics) the Faculty of Computer Science. Candidates are admitted through the **Szkoła Doktorska ZZOK**.

Topics actively supervised in 2026/27 include:
- Predictive coding in early sensory cortex (Krzemińska)
- Grammatical aspect across Slavic and Baltic languages (Nowak)
- Computational modelling of skill acquisition (Krzemińska / Janicki, joint)
- Accessibility and assistive interfaces (Dąbrowski, with industry co-supervision)

PhD candidates have access to the in-house 32-channel EEG facility, the eye-tracking laboratory in room C-205, and computing resources at the consortium centre of the Poznań Supercomputing and Networking Center (PSNC) via institutional agreement.
