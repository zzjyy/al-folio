The BSc in Computer Science is the flagship undergraduate programme of the Faculty of Computer Science. It is a six-semester, 180 ECTS course delivered in English, with optional Polish-language elective modules from the second year onwards.

## Programme structure

| Year | Core modules | Electives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Discrete mathematics; Programming I & II; Linear algebra; Logic for computer scientists; English for academic purposes | — |
| 2 | Algorithms and data structures; Computer architecture; Databases; Operating systems; Probability and statistics | one cognitive-science minor module |
| 3 | Software engineering project; Distributed systems; Compilers; Final-year thesis | three electives from a list of nine |

The third-year **final-year thesis (praca licencjacka)** is supervised by a member of the academic staff and carries 20 ECTS.

## Learning outcomes

Graduates are qualified to apply for Master's programmes at any Polish or European university and are eligible for the protected professional title of *inżynier* (engineer) on successful completion of an additional one-semester engineering project.

## Career destinations

Recent BSc graduates have taken up positions at Atos, Allegro, ING Tech Poland, the Polish National Bank IT division, and small software houses in the Tri-City. About a third of each cohort continues directly into our MSc programme.
