A part-time *studia podyplomowe* programme for working professionals who wish to formalise their training in data engineering. Classes are held on Saturdays (in person) and Wednesday evenings (online).

## Curriculum
- Modern data warehousing and lakehouses
- Stream processing (Kafka, Flink)
- Data quality, observability, lineage
- Cloud data platforms (vendor-neutral)
- Capstone project with an external sponsor (60 hours)

The certificate carries 60 ECTS and is recognised under the Polish Qualifications Framework at level 7. Employers may apply for partial co-financing through the Polish *Krajowy Fundusz Szkoleniowy*. Cohorts are limited to 25 participants.
