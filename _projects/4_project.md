A two-year, 120-ECTS taught Master's offered jointly by the Faculty of Computer Science and the Faculty of Cognitive Studies. The programme has run since 2020 and admits roughly 35 students per year.

## Curriculum

The first year covers the foundations of statistical learning, deep learning, optimisation, and the responsible-AI module *Audit, bias and uncertainty*. Students complete a 30-ECTS research thesis in the second year, supervised by a member of one of the two faculties.

## Tracks
- **Modelling and inference** — probabilistic methods, Bayesian deep learning, calibration.
- **Cognitive AI** — embodied agents, language and reasoning, alignment with human cognition.
- **AI for systems** — ML for systems, performance prediction, ML-driven optimisation.

## Entry requirements

A Bachelor's degree in computer science, mathematics, cognitive science, physics or a closely related discipline. Evidence of programming proficiency (a portfolio is preferred) and B2+/C1 English are required.

Funded research-thesis topics for 2026/27 are listed at <code>https://zzok.edu.pl/admissions/msc-ai-topics/</code>.
