about

history, mission, governance, and external accreditation of ZZOK College.

Mission

ZZOK exists to advance the rigorous study of computation and cognition, to teach those subjects to a small but ambitious student body, and to translate the resulting knowledge into open, public goods. We are a teaching institution that takes research seriously, and a research institution that takes teaching seriously.

A short history

The college was founded in 2006 by a group of academics who had previously held appointments at the University of Szczecin and the West Pomeranian University of Technology, with the support of the city of Szczecin and the West Pomeranian Voivodeship. The founding charter — signed at the former marine-engineering offices on ul. Słoneczna — committed the new institution to three principles: small cohorts, an open scientific culture, and a deliberately interdisciplinary curriculum bridging computer science and the cognitive sciences.

ZZOK was admitted to the national register of non-public higher-education institutions in 2007 (entry no. 376) and received its first programmatic accreditation from the Polish Accreditation Committee (PKA) in 2010. The Faculty of Cognitive Studies was added in 2012; the Department of Applied Machine Learning was spun out from the Faculty of Computer Science in 2019. The college joined the Erasmus+ programme in 2014 and currently maintains 41 inter-institutional agreements across Europe.

Governance

ZZOK is governed by a Rector, an elected Senate of nine members, and two faculty Deans. The current term of office runs 2024–2028.

Accreditation and external recognition

  • Polish Accreditation Committee (PKA) — institutional and programmatic accreditation, most recent review 2023.
  • Ministry of Science and Higher Education — register entry no. 376.
  • European University Association (EUA) — institutional member since 2018.
  • Erasmus Charter for Higher Education 2021–2027 — signatory.
  • PRES (Performance-based Research Funding System) — Faculty of Computer Science holds category A; Faculty of Cognitive Studies holds category B+.
