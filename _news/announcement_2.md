The National Science Centre (Narodowe Centrum Nauki) has awarded an **OPUS-22** grant to Prof. dr hab. Tomasz Wieczorek for the project *Proof complexity of constraint-satisfaction problems over finite structures*. The grant runs for three years (2026–2029) with a total budget of **1 287 400 PLN** and will fund two postdoctoral positions and two doctoral stipends. Recruitment for the postdoctoral positions opens in June 2026; details will be posted on the [research page](/research/) and on EURAXESS.

The project continues a line of work that ZZOK has pursued since 2018, in collaboration with groups at the University of Cambridge (DAMTP) and ETH Zürich. Two of the four positions are reserved for candidates trained outside Poland, in keeping with the Centre's internationalisation expectations.
