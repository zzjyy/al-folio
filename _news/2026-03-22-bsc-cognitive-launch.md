The Polish Accreditation Committee (PKA) has granted programmatic accreditation to the new **BSc in Cognitive Science**, with effect from the 2026/27 academic year. The decision follows a two-year preparatory process led by Dr hab. Aleksandra Nowak (Dean of the Faculty of Cognitive Studies) and a site visit by the PKA's expert team in November 2025.

The programme will admit its first cohort of **40 students** in October 2026. Twelve of those places are reserved for international candidates and four are tied to the Voivodeship Scholarship for International Students. Applications open on **1 June 2026** through the [IRK ZZOK](https://irk.zzok.edu.pl) portal.

> "We have spent twenty years teaching computer science alongside cognitive subjects without giving cognitive science a dedicated undergraduate programme of its own. That changes now," said Dr Nowak.
