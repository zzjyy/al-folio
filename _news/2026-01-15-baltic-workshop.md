ZZOK invites submissions to the **11th Baltic Workshop on Computation and Mind**, to be held in Szczecin on **8–10 September 2026**. The workshop welcomes contributions from across cognitive science, theoretical computer science, philosophy of mind and machine learning, with the unifying theme *Models that explain, not only predict*.

## Important dates
- Abstract submission: 30 April 2026
- Notification of acceptance: 15 June 2026
- Final camera-ready: 10 July 2026
- Workshop: 8–10 September 2026

## Keynote speakers
- Prof. Anna Mucha-Sliwerska — University of Warsaw
- Prof. Henrik Lindqvist — Linköping University
- Prof. Maja Mataric — University of Southern California (online)

Submissions are made via OpenReview at <code>https://openreview.net/group?id=zzok.edu.pl/BWCM/2026</code>. Travel grants are available for advanced doctoral students; see [research](/research/) for details.

Workshop chairs: Krzemińska, Wieczorek (ZZOK).
